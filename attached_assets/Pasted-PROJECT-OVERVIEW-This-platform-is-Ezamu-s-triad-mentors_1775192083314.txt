PROJECT OVERVIEW:

This platform is Ezamu’s triad mentorship system that includes a student, coach, and a peer. This is a website for students in high school, college, or even young adults looking for guidance in improving a specific skill or achieving a S.M.A.R.T. goal they’ve created for themselves. This website will allow them to connect with a coach that will guide and motivate them to achieve goals that they will set. This platform will also include a peer/accountability partner system, where other students can match with other students and be a peer that motivates them and keeps them on track to their tasks. A family member/guardian will also be able to make an account to monitor how their child is doing. 

DATABASE SETUP:

The database should be called ezamu_db. There should be __ tables consisting of:

Users Table: should have columns for their user id, their first and last name, their email, their password (hashed), their role (student, coach, guardian), profile pic url, their inner hero archetype, coach they are matched with, list of guardians allowed to view their progress, the peer id that they are matched with, student bio, student age, student list of fields of interests, coach bio, coach age (optional), coach fields of expertise.

Appointment Table: columns with appointment id, student id, coach id, peer id (optional), title, scheduled date and time.

Action Items Table: columns with action item id, student id, title, description, completed (boolean).

Coach Availability Table: coach id, date, start time, end time.

Assessment Results Table: id, student id, inner hero type (between Helper, Doer, Thinker, and Planner), score for each type (should add up to 100), date taken, summary (string of what this result means).

USERS & USE CASES:

Students will be able to go on this website, sign up for an account, and have access to a dashboard that shows their upcoming appointments, a list of actionable items they have to do assigned by their coach, and their latest assessment results. When they initially sign up for an account, they will be led to a page after successful sign up that asks them to fill in basic information question by question, these questions are how old are you, what fields are you interested in, and write a short bio about yourself. There will also be clickable cards that are labeled SMART Goals and Your Team. SMART Goals leads to a page where they are able to create their SMART Goals for the coach to review and approve of, there will also be a progress tracker along with actionable items linked to these goals. The Your Team card will lead them to a page where they are able to see who makes up their Triad team, so their coach information, peer information, and parent information. Students can generate an invite link sent to their guardian here. Students are also peers, so if they have an existing peer, they are able to click on their peer card information, and it will lead to their profile that shows more details about their peer and the list of actionable items they have. 

Coaches will be able to sign up for an account, upon successful completion, they will be led to a page that asks basic information such as how old they are (allow a skip button), fields of expertise, write a short bio of yourself. They will be led to a coach dashboard where they will see a list of upcoming appointments and a list of students they are actively guiding. They can click on the student’s name to be led to a page that shows the student’s information, their progress, and SMART goals that they approve or deny. They can also write a note about the student only they can see to keep track of any information they want to know for the next meetings. 

Guardians will be able to login with an invite from their child, upon login they are able to see the student’s dashboard but they cannot edit anything on it.
